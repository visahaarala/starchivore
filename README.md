# Starchivore

[https://starchivore.singingtrumpet.com](https://starchivore.singingtrumpet.com)

This simple foods explorer offers one perspective of what the McDougall starchivore diet might mean. Turning on the starchivore toggle filters away foods with fiber/sugar ratio of less than 0.3 and fiber/fat ratio of less than 0.45. The result is mostly non-starchy vegetables for health and starchy vegetables for energy, as well as a little bit of high-fiber seeds, fruits and berries.

Only foods with scientific name, raw, and fiber > 0 are included.  
Plus all the seeds (not raw in the Fineli database).

Website in vanilla html, css & javascript. Data manipulation in typescript.  
Deployed at Vercel.

Source of data is Peruspaketti 1 (Basic package 1) from Fineli:  
[https://fineli.fi/fineli/fi/avoin-data](https://fineli.fi/fineli/fi/avoin-data)
